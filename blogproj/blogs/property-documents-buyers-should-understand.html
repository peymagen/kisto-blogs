FLAT DOCUMENTATION GUIDE · SEPTEMBER 2026

Property Documents Buyers Should Understand

A flat may look perfect during a site visit, but a confident purchase also depends on understanding the paperwork. This guide highlights the document checks buyers should discuss before moving ahead.

KISTO SONS

Property documents help establish the rights, approvals and transaction terms connected with a property. They should be considered alongside the physical inspection and price discussion.

The exact documents depend on the property, location and transaction. Ask for the records that apply specifically to the flat you are considering.

Confirm Ownership and Property Details

One of the first questions is who has the legal right to sell or transfer the property. Buyers should also check that important property details are consistent across the records provided.

If names, area, identifiers or other important details do not match, pause and ask for a clear explanation before proceeding.

Check Relevant Approvals and Project Records

For a new flat or development, ask about the approvals, registrations and project records applicable to that property.

Do not rely only on a brochure or verbal explanation when an important point can be checked through the relevant record or an appropriate professional source.

Read the Agreement and Payment Terms

Before signing, understand the flat description, price, payment schedule, possession or handover conditions, inclusions, responsibilities and other important terms.

Keep copies of signed documents, payment receipts and important written communications in one place for future reference.

Clarify Outstanding Charges

Ask whether there are maintenance dues, utility balances, taxes, loans or other charges connected with the property, and clarify who is responsible for them.

The responsibility for any outstanding amount should be clear in the transaction documents before you commit.

Use Professional Verification When Needed

Property paperwork can be technical. If the ownership history is complicated or a clause is unclear, qualified legal advice can help you understand the issue.

Kisto Sons can provide the property information available to it, while buyers should independently verify important legal and financial details for their specific transaction.

Ready to Discuss a Flat?

If you are interested in a specific flat, ask for its current price, availability, configuration and relevant property documents before making a booking decision.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a brochure replace property documents?

No. A brochure is marketing material. Important ownership, approval and transaction details should be verified through the relevant records.

Should I keep copies of the agreement?

Yes. Keep copies of signed documents, receipts and important written communication so you have a clear record of the transaction.

Who should I ask about legal questions?

For legal verification or complex clauses, consult a qualified legal professional rather than relying on an informal explanation.
