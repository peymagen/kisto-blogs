LOCATION FOR FLAT BUYERS · SEPTEMBER 2026

How Connectivity Can Affect Property Choice

When you buy a flat, you are choosing more than the building—you are choosing a daily route. Road access, public transport and the places you visit regularly can make a major difference to how convenient a location feels.

KISTO SONS

List the places you travel to most: work, school, healthcare, markets, family destinations and transport hubs. Then check the real routes from each shortlisted property.

A location can look convenient on a map and still feel different during busy hours. Practical travel time is often more useful than a simple distance measurement.

Check Road Access and Parking

Visit the approach road and the final stretch to the property. Narrow roads, traffic bottlenecks, turning space and parking pressure can become everyday concerns.

If parking matters to you, confirm the arrangement that applies to the specific flat instead of assuming that parking shown in a photograph is automatically included.

Look at Public Transport Options

If you use bus, metro or rail services, check the practical walking and road connection to the relevant stop or station. Drivers should also consider the routes they will use most often.

Having more than one practical route can be useful when traffic or transport conditions change. Compare routes at the times you are most likely to travel.

Nearby Facilities Add Everyday Value

Healthcare, schools, grocery stores, pharmacies, banks and other services can matter just as much as the distance to a workplace when you are choosing a home.

Walk or drive around the neighbourhood during a site visit. Seeing the surroundings yourself can give you a clearer picture than a property listing alone.

Visit at a Useful Time

Traffic, noise and parking can change throughout the day. If possible, visit at a time that reflects your normal routine.

This gives you more realistic information about the location and helps you compare properties using your actual daily needs.

Compare Location Alongside the Flat

When comparing two flats, record travel time, road access, nearby facilities, transport options and parking conditions alongside price and layout.

Then compare those location factors with the flat's size, configuration, price and other requirements before creating your final shortlist.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a good location enough to choose a flat?

Location is important, but it should be considered together with layout, price, documents, condition and your family's daily routine.

Should I check traffic before buying?

Yes, especially if your routine depends on specific roads or transport. Visit at a relevant time and check the actual journey.

Can I request a site visit?

You can contact the property team to discuss available properties, current details and the next steps for a site visit.
